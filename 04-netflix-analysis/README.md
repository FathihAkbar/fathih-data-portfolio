# Netflix Movies & TV Shows Analysis

## Overview

Analysis of Netflix Movies and TV Shows based on content type, year added, country, rating, genre, and duration.

## Objective

Analyze:

- Movie vs TV Show distribution
- Content growth over time
- Country distribution
- Ratings
- Genres
- Duration

## Dataset

Source: Kaggle – Netflix Movies and TV Shows

## Tools

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Key Results

- Movies represented approximately 69.62% of the dataset.
- TV Shows represented approximately 30.38%.
- 2019 had the highest number of titles added.
- United States was the most common primary country.
- TV-MA was the most common rating.
- International Movies was the most frequently listed genre.
